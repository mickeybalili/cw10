# Metaphors

The best way to learn how metaphors work is by listening to songs because music strips imagery down to its purest emotional rhythm, demonstrating how two completely unrelated concepts can fuse to create instant meaning. 

In this playlist, listen to how lyricists select, sustain, and distort imagery within the tight constraints of a melody: active, living engines of emotion and narrative tension.
