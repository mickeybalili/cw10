# THe CW 10 Book Club

This is the Creative Non-fiction part of CW 10 in which you will read an entire book and write about/around it. This is also your Midterm paper which constitutes 20% of your grade.

| Title | Author | Genre | What is it about? | 3 Keywords |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Autobiography of Red | Anne Carson | Novel-in-verse | Poetic novel reimagining myth. Fragmented structure and imagery tell the story. | poetry, myth, love |
| Bluets | Maggie Nelson | Autotheory/Poetry/CNF | Lyrical fragments reflecting on love, loss, and the color blue. See how short pieces create depth. | color, emotion, fragments |
| Bunny | Mona Awad | Novel | A darkly comic and surreal novel about a scholarship student who joins an elite MFA program, where she encounters a clique of strange, eerie classmates called the “Bunnies.” | dark comedy, surreal, identity |
| Convenience Store Woman | Sayaka Murata | Novel | Quiet novel about identity through routine and work. Repetition builds character subtly. | routine, identity, society |
| Crying in H Mart | Michelle Zauner | CNF | Memoir about grief, food, and identity. Specific moments make emotion feel personal. | grief, identity, food |
| Dark Hours | Conchitina Cruz | Poetry | Poems reflecting on memory, family, and loss. Concise lines and vivid details create intimacy. | memory, family, loss |
| Department of Speculation | Jenny Offill | Novel in fragments | Marriage told in short, reflective fragments. Form shapes the experience. | marriage, reflection, fragments |
| Don’t Let Me Be Lonely | Claudia Rankine | Poetry/CNF | Poetry and essay hybrid on isolation and grief. Lyric and prose evoke emotion in short bursts. | grief, isolation, lyric |
| Edinburgh | Alexander Chee | Novel | Coming-of-age novel about trauma and survival. Internal reflection and detailed scenes build depth. | trauma, identity, growth |
| Fun Home | Alison Bechdel | Graphic Novel | Graphic memoir about family, identity, and memory. Text and images work together to tell story. | memoir, family, identity |
| Ghost World | Daniel Clowes | Graphic Novel | Graphic novel about adolescence, friendship, and alienation. Visual storytelling and dialogue show character. | adolescence, friendship, graphic |
| Happily Ever Ek-ek | Paolo Manalo | Poetry | A playful, inventive poetry collection that twists the idea of “happily ever after” with ek‑ek—a Filipino slang for nonsense—exploring love, everyday life, pop culture, and longing through poems that mix traditional forms (like sonnets and tanaga) with references to video games, teleseryes, and popular songs. | Filipino culture, experimental, love/longing |
| How to Write an Autobiographical Novel | Alexander Chee | CNF | Memoir and craft essay. Life and art inform each other, blending reflection and storytelling. | memoir, craft, reflection |
| If on a Winter’s Night a Traveler | Italo Calvino | Novel | This novel plays with the idea of reading itself. It alternates between the story of “you,” the reader, and the beginnings of multiple different novels. | metafiction, reading, experimental |
| Just Kids | Patti Smith | CNF | Memoir of youth, art, and friendship. Small anecdotes build character and mood. | art, friendship, memoir |
| Love and Information | Caryl Churchill | Play | Play of short scenes and fragments. Dialogue and minimal directions create ideas without exposition. | play, fragments, dialogue |
| Self-Help | Lorrie Moore | Short Story Collection | Stories mixing humor and heartbreak. Witty surprises reveal character in small moments. | humor, heartbreak, character |
| The Beauty of the Husband | Anne Carson | Poetry | Verse novel about love and betrayal. Concise lines and metaphor carry complex emotions. | love, betrayal, poetry |
| The Harpy | Megan Hunter | Novel | Spare novel imagining life after loss. Each line carries emotional weight with minimal imagery. | loss, transformation, mythic |
| The Left Hand of Darkness | Ursula K. Le Guin | Novel | Sci-fi novel exploring gender and society. World-building and character observation create meaning. | sci-fi, gender, society |
| The Vegetarian | Han Kang | Novel | Novel about transformation, desire, and society. Small imagery conveys emotional tension. | transformation, desire, society |
| The Wild Iris | Louise Glück | Poetry | Nature-inspired poems exploring life, death, and voice. Minimal language creates depth. | nature, life, voice |
| We, the Animals | Justin Torres | Novel | Short, vivid scenes of three brothers growing up. Notice how small moments build mood and emotion. | childhood, memory, family |
| Written on the Body | Jeanette Winterson | Novel | Poetic, gender-ambiguous love story. Intimate imagery conveys desire without naming the narrator. | love, body, desire |
