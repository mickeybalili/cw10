## Consultation

- You may pitch your ideas to your instructor, or go over your concepts, before committing them on paper. Think of this opportunity as a place to fine-tune ideas and concepts before writing your draft. Take advantage of it.

- There will also be time set aside during the semester when you can schedule a one-on-one consultation with your instructor. The consultation can be through Zoom or Discord, or if you are unable to commit to a synchronous meeting, through email or just through Discord. 

- During the consultation, your instructor may check up on you and discuss your performance in class, or inquire if you have been having difficulties with the sessions or readings. However, the bulk of your discussion will be about your plans for the workshop draft submission.
