## KALBaryo

Since the Faculty Center burned down 10 years ago (imagine that!), CAL still has no building to call its own. I am heartbroken to tell you we won't have physical spaces to hold consultations. We can do this through Zoom or Discord (please email and schedule) or informally, after class while we're walking down the staircase (elevator-pitch style) or if you want a longer kwentuhan, inside the Gonzalo Gonzales Reading Room (again, send an email and schedule).

## Consultation

- You may pitch your ideas or go over your concepts to me before committing them on paper. Think of this opportunity as a place to fine-tune ideas & concepts before writing your draft. Take advantage of it.

- There will also be time set aside during the semester when you can schedule a one-on-one consultation with me. The consultation can be through Zoom or Discord, or if you are unable to commit to a synchronous meeting, through email or through Discord.

- During the consultation, I may check up on you & discuss your performance in class, or inquire if you have been having difficulties with the sessions or readings. However, the bulk of your discussion will be about your plans for the workshop draft submission.
